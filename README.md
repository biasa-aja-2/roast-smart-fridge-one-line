## The line

> My smart fridge sent me a weekly health report, locked the ice dispenser, and still couldn't tell me where the leftovers went — it just watched me eat them.

156 characters (limit 160).

## Why this line

Three specific, real smart-fridge behaviours stack up — the weekly health report,
the locked ice dispenser — and then the punchline flips the fridge's omniscience
against itself: for all its surveillance it cannot find the leftovers, because it
was too busy watching me eat them. It is not the stock joke about a fridge that
orders too much milk; it is a fridge that acts like a judgemental health coach
while failing at the one job a fridge actually has: knowing what is inside it.

`main.py` (pure Python stdlib) writes the line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`
(default `/output/entry.txt`), checks the 1–160 character gate, and verifies the
output byte-for-byte by reading it back.
